Strawberry farming is one of the most climate-sensitive specialty crops in agriculture. Unlike many grains and row crops, strawberries require narrow temperature, moisture, and harvest windows to maintain both yield and fruit quality. Even a few days of extreme heat, heavy rainfall, hail, or frost can significantly affect yields.
